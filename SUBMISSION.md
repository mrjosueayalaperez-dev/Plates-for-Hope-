## One-line description
A bilingual meal-planning coordinator that turns ingredients on hand, a grocery budget, a headcount, and dietary needs into a scaled menu, a split grocery list with a budget verdict, and a volunteer checklist in serving order.

## Problem solved
Community meal organizers plan under three hard constraints at once — donated ingredients, little cash, and guests with real dietary restrictions and allergies. The Coordinator does the arithmetic and the sequencing in one pass: it spends on-hand ingredients first, buys only what is missing, refuses to violate a stated restriction silently, flags allergy concerns for organizer review, and tells the truth when the budget cannot feed the headcount.

## Walkthrough
1. Enter ingredients on hand with quantities (e.g., chicken thighs 8 lb, rice 10 lb).
2. Set the budget and currency, the headcount, dietary restrictions, allergies, and cooking equipment.
3. Tap **Build my meal plan**. Get: the menu scaled to the headcount; the grocery list split into "You already have" / "To buy" with estimated costs; an UNDER/OVER budget verdict with the difference; and the volunteer checklist in serving order.
4. Edit any input and rebuild, **Copy** the whole plan as text, or **Save** it to reopen later. Toggle **Español** at any point — the whole experience, including a saved plan's labels, is bilingual.

## Truthful project story
Plates for Hope has served approximately 45 meals through the founder's direct community efforts. Those meals were not coordinated through this app. Regular service is a future goal. No results are promised beyond what the test evidence shows.

## Test evidence (summary — full matrix in TEST-EVIDENCE.md)
Milestone test matrix passed Oct 26–27, 2026: budget/headcount arithmetic verified by direct engine calls and live runs; tight-budget OVER case with cheapest-fix guidance; vegetarian + gluten-free satisfaction; allergy flagging with organizer-review banner; currency handling (estimates in the chosen currency, never converted); invalid-input rejection (including a negative-quantity hole caught by audit, fixed, and retested); an honest failure path that saves nothing; EN/ES parity drives; mobile layout pass.

## Limitations
Prices are planning estimates, not store prices. Allergen safety is not guaranteed; organizer review is required on flagged plans. The Meta Model API layer is pending the founder's credit claim. The dish library is small by design.
